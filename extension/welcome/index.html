Ebb keeps your tabs calm

Tabs you leave alone ebb away in stages. You never lose a page, and you never have to sort anything.

  1. Resting

    After a while unused, a tab is unloaded in place. It stays in your tab bar and reloads when you click it.

  2. Shelf

    Later, it moves into a collapsed Shelf group at the end of the tab bar. One click brings it back. In a window with more than 15 tabs, the ones you used least recently move here sooner, while you’re away.

  3. Library

    Much later, it closes into Ebb’s Library with your place on the page and a snippet of its text. Type ebb and a space in the address bar to find it and pick up where you were.

Only active time counts

The clock runs only while you are using Chrome, so a weekend away doesn’t empty your tabs. Tabs you keep coming back to stay much longer.

Some tabs are never touched

The tab you’re on, pinned tabs, tabs playing sound, tabs with unsaved typing, and web apps like mail, calendar and chat.

Tasks, not piles

Ebb groups tabs by what you were doing: a search, or a site you went to. When a confirmation page says you’re done, it offers to tuck that task away.

A Later lane

When one page spawns a pile of background tabs, the extra links wait in a Later list instead of crowding your tab bar.
